Two ways to buy

Quick retail purchase, or standing institutional supply.

Most of what we do falls into one of two lanes — walk in and buy for the day, or set up a recurring supply arrangement for a kitchen. Both are billed properly.

Retail counter

Walk in, pick what you need, get a printed bill on the spot. Rates are the day's mandi rate.

Home delivery

Standing daily or alternate-day delivery for households and small restaurants nearby.

Bulk institutional supply

Factories, canteens, schools and colleges get a fixed rate for the period, a set delivery schedule, and an A4 GST invoice per order.

Custom sorting & weighing

Large orders are pre-sorted and weighed into crates before dispatch, so kitchen staff can start prep immediately.

How institutional orders work

1. Share requirement

Fill the bulk order form with your organisation, items and delivery date.

2. Rate confirmed

We confirm quantities and rate for your order size before dispatch.

3. Delivered on schedule

Order is sorted, weighed and delivered on the agreed date and time.

4. Invoice issued

A proper A4 invoice is generated for your records and accounts team.
