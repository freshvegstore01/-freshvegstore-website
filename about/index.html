Our story

A mandi stall that grew into a proper supply business.

FreshVegStore started as a single vegetable stall at VPO Asron. Over the years, nearby households started asking for regular home delivery — and soon after, canteens and hostel kitchens started asking for weekly supply on a bill they could keep. Today we run both sides of that business from the same stall: quick retail billing for walk-in customers, and a dedicated bulk desk for institutions.

Where we are

VPO Asron, near SBI Bank, Punjab – 144533. Open every day except major festivals.

How we source

Stock is picked from the local mandi and nearby farms each morning, based on what's fresh that day — not a fixed catalogue.

Who we serve

Households buying daily, restaurants and hostels on standing orders, and factories, canteens, schools and colleges on bulk supply.

What guides the business

Fewer promises, kept consistently.

Same-day freshness

Nothing sold today was picked more than a day earlier. If it isn't fresh, it isn't on the crate.

A bill for every sale

Whether it's a ₹200 retail purchase or a bulk canteen order, every sale is billed and recorded — no loose accounting.

Rates you can plan around

Bulk clients get a quoted rate for the agreed period instead of the daily mandi swing, so kitchen budgeting stays simple.

Delivery that shows up

Standing supply clients get a fixed delivery window — the same days, the same time, every week.

Want to see today's rates or place a bulk order?

Retail rates are confirmed at billing; bulk rates are quoted for your order size.
